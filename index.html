Choose how you’d like to begin

The AI assistant can ask one question at a time. It is not a human counselor and may make mistakes. You can stop at any time.

If you choose AI, your messages and conversation history are sent to this app’s server and its configured Azure service. Avoid sharing Social Security numbers, account numbers, or other information you do not want processed.

Optional voice input

You can type, or you can use your browser’s microphone feature. Depending on your browser, audio may be sent to its speech provider for transcription. This app does not upload audio to its own server. Voice input may not be available on every device.
